# WhatsApp Agent Kit in 60 seconds

*(Read aloud in about a minute. The setup itself takes about 10 minutes.)*

This kit turns a WhatsApp chat into your own AI assistant, running on your computer, with whichever AI you like.

**One.** On your phone, open WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Agents, and create an agent. Open its chat info and copy the API key.

**Two.** Unzip this folder on a computer that stays on. Open your AI helper, whether that's Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini or another, point it at this folder and say: *"Set this up for me."* It reads the instructions inside and does the rest with you.

**Three.** When it asks, paste your WhatsApp key into the Terminal window it opens. Your key goes straight into your computer's safe storage, never into the chat.

**Four.** Send your agent a message on WhatsApp. You'll see it typing, then your answer.

That's it. Files, photos and voice notes work too. Change how it behaves any time by editing one file: INSTRUCTIONS.md.
